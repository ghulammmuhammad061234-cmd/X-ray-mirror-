🧠 NeuroSight AI

X-Ray Mirror – Human Anatomy & Movement Visualization System

See Beyond the Surface, Explore Human Anatomy.

NeuroSight AI is an interactive educational project that uses camera-based tracking technology to explore selected human facial and hand movements through digital visualization.

It combines face detection, smile detection, hand tracking, anatomy visualization, and educational neuron simulations in one platform.

---

🚀 Features

1. Face Detection & Tracking

- Live face detection.
- Facial landmark tracking.
- Smile detection.
- Facial movement visualization.
- Educational facial muscle visualization.

2. Hand Tracking

- Live hand tracking.
- Finger movement detection.
- Hand landmark visualization.
- Educational hand skeleton and bone visualization.

3. Brain & Neuron Education

- Brain anatomy information.
- Animated neuron signal simulation.
- Educational explanations of the nervous system.

4. Interactive Learning

- Anatomy information.
- Science questions and answers.
- Interactive quizzes.
- Learning progress tracking.

▶️ How to Use

1. Open the NeuroSight AI website.
2. Allow camera permission when requested.
3. Start the face or hand tracking feature.
4. Explore the anatomy visualizations.
5. Learn about facial movements, hand structure, and neurons.
6. Complete the science quizzes.

Note: Camera access generally requires HTTPS or localhost.

---

🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of NeuroSight AI is to make human anatomy and neuroscience easier to understand through interactive technology.

It aims to connect computer science with biology and educational visualization.

---

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

NeuroSight AI is an educational visualization project.

- It is NOT a real medical X-ray machine.
- It does NOT see inside the human body.
- Its anatomy overlays are digital educational visualizations.
- Neuron activity is simulated for learning purposes.
- It is NOT intended for medical diagnosis.
